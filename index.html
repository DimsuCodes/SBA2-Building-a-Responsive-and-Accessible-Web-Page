About Us

Accessible Design Co. is a small team of designers and developers who believe the web should work for everyone. Since 2018, we've helped businesses, nonprofits, and schools reach more people by making their sites easier to see, hear, and navigate.

Accessibility isn't an add-on for us. It's built into every project from the first sketch to the final line of code.
